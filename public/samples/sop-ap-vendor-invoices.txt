Vendor invoice processing – Client: Arvind Retail.
1. Download invoices daily from the shared AP inbox.
2. Check PO number, GST number and amount against the PO in SAP.
3. If amount matches, post the invoice in SAP and mark 'Ready for payment'.
4. If amount differs, send to the client AP lead for approval.
5. Payment run happens every Thursday; invoices posted after Wednesday 6 PM go to next week.
6. Urgent vendors are paid in the same week.
7. Month-end: send the open-invoice report to the client controller by working day 2.
